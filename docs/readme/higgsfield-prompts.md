## The mark: one brass key on its hook

- **Use:** a square product mark for bwtidy, used as the repository's social avatar or as a small mark beside the label-maker wordmark. It is never used as a screenshot or as output.
- **Model:** Higgsfield Soul (soul_2), prompt only. This follows the standing rule to stay in the Soul family. A photoreal macro shot of one object is what Soul is good at, and the prompt asks for no lettering, so text rendering does not matter. The wordmark stays as code, on label-maker tape.
- **Aspect:** 1:1. Generate 4 options and pick 1.

**One idea.** The whole product in one object: a single brass key hanging, tidy, on its own steel hook.

**Style lock.** Matches docs/readme/IDENTITY.md: a mint powder-coated steel panel (`#b6e2cd`), brushed steel hardware, a warm brass key (`#d6a64a`), soft light from the top left, a shadow falling down and to the right.
